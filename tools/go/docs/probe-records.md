In-flight work can finish after the elapsed cap; enforcement occurs at cooperative ticks, not through a kernel timer. Each completed acquisition/publication attempt schedules its next attempt after four seconds, preserving the enclosing wait's deadline. A cold owned-ready epoch resets its comparison predecessor while run time, retained ordinals and budgets continue. These are source-defined software policies, not an accepted native soak or resource agreement.

Journal creation uses the generic writer and one journal commit. Selected-frame state advances only after JPEG, CSV and event-journal acknowledgements. Failed or uncertain writes retain reservations and pending facts; no retry may silently replace a partial transaction. Verified observation failures can retain an accounted native input, while authority/resource failures remain fatal to the sampler. Managed success unlinks only its verified native input; the public raw capture operation retains its original PNG contract.

Managed artifact accounting observes owned identities and reserves native/working exposure plus request-derived finalizer headroom.
